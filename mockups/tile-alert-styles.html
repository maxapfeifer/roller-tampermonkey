Built — family name-duplicate override (v5.168)

The chosen direction: for a family membership where the booker’s name is stamped on multiple tickets (so a child ticket mismatches its own pass), the hard mismatch is replaced by this softer nudge — the add-photo box format with a one-tap override. Genuine cross-account mismatches keep the hard lock (card 4).
